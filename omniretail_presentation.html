Key Insights

Revenue leadership

West region drives the business

West generated $178K in revenue with an average order value of $1,286. It is the strongest region across both total revenue and transaction quality, and should be the benchmark for other regions.

Category strength

Electronics and Home account for 84% of revenue

Together they generated $506K out of $602K total. Electronics has the highest average sale at $2,631 per transaction, making it the most valuable category to grow and protect.

Requires attention

Store 6 is underperforming on both fronts

Store 6 completed 215 transactions but generated only $35K in revenue, the lowest of all stores. It also has the highest return rate at 18%. Both issues together suggest something is wrong at this location and it needs investigation.

Seasonal opportunity

December is the strongest month by far

December generated $67.7K, more than double January's $28.3K. Q3 and Q4 are consistently the strongest periods. Planning inventory and staffing around this pattern could significantly improve efficiency.

Product quality flag

Nearly one in three Bed Frames is returned

The Bed Frame has a return rate of 30.8%, well above the company average of 9.8%. This level of returns is costly and suggests a product quality, description, or assembly problem that should be reviewed immediately.

Star product

Smart Watch is the top revenue product

The Smart Watch generated $48.9K in revenue and was the best selling product in two out of four regions. Keeping it well stocked and visible across all stores would have a direct impact on overall revenue.
